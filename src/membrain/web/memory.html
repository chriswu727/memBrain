Your memory as a brain

        
Each dot is one memory, placed in the area for its kind. Lines are links that memory
          records, such as a note and the message it came from. The layout is illustrative: dot size is not memory
          strength. The brightest dots are the newest.

The gold ring marks only the records the latest recorded message recall returned. It is held
            in memory until the service restarts and is not everything a reply used.

          
Size is the map's layout weight: a pinned note is drawn larger. It is not memory strength,
            confidence or importance. Colour is the kind of record, as in the list beside the map.

          
The brain-shaped shell is an illustrative container, not neuroscience. Its areas are kinds of
            record, not parts of a real brain, and the dots are software records, not neurons.

        

        

          Graph coverage
          
Memories shows notes, original messages and attached feelings. Companion development shows
            personality feedback and decisions, simulated mood triggers, relationship settings and commitments.
            Both are bounded inspection windows, not everything a reply used.

          
    
            
  • Learned memory Assertions, their revisions and
              sources. Available in Memories.
    • 
            
  • Episodes and associations Episode membership and typed
              links between memories. Available in Memories. “Recent messages” are original messages, not
              episodes.
    • 
            
  • Personality development Versions, suggestions and your
              decisions. Available in Companion development.
    • 
            
  • Simulated mood Triggers and the baseline. Available in
              Companion development.
    • 
            
  • Relationship evidence Support style, boundaries,
              promises and familiarity limits. Available in Companion development; complete familiarity
              aggregates are not included yet.

    Notes
    
        
    Lasting facts your agent keeps about you. Every session starts with these. Correct anything
          that is wrong; your corrections take precedence over the agent's notes.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      Add a note
      
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Personality
    
        
    How your companion presents itself: its name, character, voice, values and boundaries.
          Your agent receives it in its own block, apart from what it remembers about you. Every save keeps the
          earlier versions. How you like replies, as your agent learns it, stays in your preference notes.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Current card
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Suggestions from your feedback
    
      
    When you give your companion the same kind of feedback on how it talks three times, in any
        conversations, memBrain suggests a change to its card here and quotes what you said. Nothing changes until
        you accept. If you reject a suggestion, none of the same kind is offered again for 30 days.
    
      
    
      
    
        Earlier suggestions
        
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Simulated mood
    
      
    A short-term mood your companion simulates from what happens in your conversations. It is not a
        real feeling and says nothing about you. It drifts back to the baseline mood on its own; time apart never
        changes it.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
      
    
        Triggers
        
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
        Voice
        
        
        
        
      
    
      
      
      
    
        Baseline mood
        
        
        
        
    Leave the mood empty to rest at calm. It is the resting point the simulated mood returns to;
          it is not a feeling.
    
      
    
      
      
    The card is limited to 1,200 characters. Values that look like passwords or keys are removed.
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Versions
    
      
    Newest first. Restoring a version saves its content as a new version.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Delete personality history
    
      
    Erases every version, including the current card. Deletion is recorded, so it also holds if
        an older backup is restored. Notes and messages are not touched.
    
      
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Relationship
    
          
    What you and your companion share: moments you had together, how familiar you are, how
            you like to be supported and the topics it should not bring up. Your agent receives it in its own block.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Familiarity
    
        
    
        
    
        
    Worked out from the days you talked and the moments you shared. Nobody sets it, and it is not
          a score of you. You can keep it at a lower level.
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    How you like to be supported
    
        
        
    Leave it empty to remove it.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Boundaries
    
        
    Topics your companion will not bring up. Earlier messages about them are left out of recall
          unless you raise the topic yourself.
    
        
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Shared moments
    
        
    Things you and your companion did or reached together, oldest first.
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Promises kept and missed
    
        
    From your agent's promise notes. A missed promise is shown to your agent once, so it can
          acknowledge it.
    
        
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Reset the relationship
    
        
    Deletes the shared moments, the support style, every boundary, the familiarity limit and the
          record of promises. Familiarity starts again at new. Conversations, your other notes, the personality and
          feelings stay.
    
        
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Plans and dates
    Open plans, upcoming dates and promises. A plan stays open until you confirm it is done.
    
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    People
    People your agent remembers, with their names and aliases.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Feelings
    
        
    Feelings you expressed, each kept with the note it belongs to and the message it came from.
          Each one says whether you said it or your agent inferred it. There is no overall score of how you feel.
          Correcting or deleting the note or its message deletes the feeling too.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Clear feelings
    
      
    Deletes every recorded feeling, said or inferred. Notes and messages stay. Feelings are never
        recreated from messages before this point.
    
      
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Conversations
    
        
    Original messages, as recorded. Linked notes show the original messages they refer to.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Search
    The same recall your agent uses before replying.
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Privacy
    Memory is stored on this computer. Recalled text is sent to the agent you use; configured model providers may also process it.
    
    
    
      
    Recording
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
        
      
    
      
    While paused nothing new is stored; recall of earlier memory keeps working. Text inside
        <private>…</private> and values that look like passwords or keys are never stored.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Export
    
      
    Download everything, with original text and sources, as one JSON file.
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
    Erase everything
    
      
    Deletes the content of every message and note. Erasure is recorded, so it also holds if an
        older backup is restored. This cannot be undone.
